# Product Vision — ModularMenu
**Working Title:** ModularMenu  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Vision Statement
ModularMenu empowers hospitality businesses—from independent bistros and specialty coffee houses to high-volume multi-brand restaurant groups—to deliver instantaneous, visually captivating, and brand-distinctive digital dining experiences through a modular, multi-tenant digital menu platform.

## 2. Market Problem
1. **Generic PDF Menus:** 80%+ of QR menus in the Indian dining ecosystem are unoptimized, heavy PDF downloads that consume mobile bandwidth, fail to scale on mobile screens, and offer zero interactivity.
2. **Rigid Aggregators:** Delivery aggregators lock restaurants into uniform, non-brandable interfaces with high commission structures and zero retention ownership.
3. **Fragile Custom Websites:** Bespoke menu websites are expensive to develop, brittle to maintain, and difficult for non-technical restaurant managers to update on the fly (e.g., 86ing an item during peak rush).
4. **Binary Hosting Lock-in:** Existing software forces restaurants either into an expensive SaaS cloud or into complex, unmaintained self-hosted scripts.

## 3. Product Solution: ModularMenu
ModularMenu solves this with a **shared multi-tenant core** and **hybrid deployment profiles**:
- **Zero-Friction Customer Experience:** Instant-loading (<1.5s LCP), zero-login mobile web menu optimized for 360px+ screens.
- **Dual-Mode Builder:** Intuitive settings forms for everyday operations, paired with a rich 3-panel live visual editor for high-touch brand styling.
- **Dynamic Theme & Layout Engine:** 6 distinct aesthetic presets (Luxury, Minimal, Rustic, Vibrant, Contemporary, Editorial) driven by design tokens rather than ad-hoc CSS.
- **Immutable Publishing Lifecycle:** Transactional snapshots that protect customer sessions from broken mid-service updates and provide one-click rollback.
- **Hybrid Delivery:** Available as a turnkey managed SaaS platform or a containerized customer-hosted Docker profile.
