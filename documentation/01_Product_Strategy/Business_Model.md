# Business Model & Commercial Strategy
**Platform:** ModularMenu  
**Architecture Model:** Hybrid Business Model (Managed SaaS + Self-Hosted Licensing)

---

## 1. Value Proposition
- **For Single-Location Cafes & Restaurants:** Instant digital QR menu with high aesthetic value, zero hosting headaches, and full control over dietary badges and stock availability.
- **For Restaurant Chains & Franchises:** Centralized organization management, multi-branch ready architecture, brand consistency, and custom domain support.
- **For Enterprise & High-Security Clients:** Customer-hosted on-premise Docker deployment running within their private cloud (AWS, GCP, bare metal) with air-gapped or dedicated database guarantees.

---

## 2. Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: Managed Platform Subscriptions (Recurring SaaS)
- **Starter Tier (₹799 / month or ₹7,999 / year):**
  - 1 Active Menu, up to 100 items.
  - Access to 3 standard theme presets (Minimal, Contemporary, Rustic).
  - Shared domain: `modularmenu.in/r/[slug]`.
  - Dynamic QR code generation & analytics basics.
- **Pro Tier (₹1,999 / month or ₹19,999 / year):**
  - Unlimited Menus (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Happy Hour schedules).
  - All 6 Theme Presets + Custom Color & Typography Tokens.
  - Full 3-panel Visual Editor.
  - Custom domain mapping (`menu.restaurantname.com`).
  - Priority support & instant cache invalidation.
- **Enterprise SaaS (Custom quote / starting at ₹4,999 / month):**
  - Multi-location organization controls.
  - Dedicated account manager, custom theme styling service.
  - Advanced analytics and POS export APIs.

### Stream 2: Customer-Hosted Deployment Licenses (Hybrid Model)
- **Annual On-Premise License (₹24,999 / year / instance):**
  - Self-hosted Docker package.
  - Cryptographically signed license key with offline verification grace period (30 days).
  - Self-managed PostgreSQL and object storage.
  - Software updates and migration scripts included for 12 months.
- **Turnkey Setup & Migration Service (₹10,000 one-time fee):**
  - Assisted installation on customer's VPS/Cloud provider.
  - Custom brand theme styling and menu digitization service.
