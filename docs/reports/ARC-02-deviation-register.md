## Deviation Policy
Any future deviation from ARC-02 principles (P-01 to P-10) or constraints (CON-01 to CON-16) must:
1. Be submitted via formal ADR using `docs/decisions/ADR-TEMPLATE.md`.
2. Include risk assessment and technical debt remediation plan.
3. Be approved by the Lead Architect and recorded in this register before code is merged.
